# Quality Boundaries

1. Golden expected responses are test-only.
2. Verbatim similarity is not the success criterion.
3. Noob, Amateur, and Pro must share the same diagnosis and experiment.
4. Noob must not be patronizing.
5. Pro must not omit decisive cues, risk, or rollback.
6. Persistent level is explicit only and cannot be inferred.
7. A turn-level request for simpler/deeper language does not persist automatically.
8. Level does not change model quality, evidence honesty, privacy, safety, or tool authority.
9. Audio claims require actual waveform receipt regardless of level.
10. This package contains no candidate Logic procedures and emits zero runtime corpus records.
