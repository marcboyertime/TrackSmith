# Review Checklist

Before promoting a candidate:

- Verify source IDs and exact source meaning.
- Distinguish official behavior, research, professional practice, and community anecdote.
- Confirm Logic path and labels in the installed version.
- Replace universal numeric language with starting-point/listen-for guidance.
- Preserve material disagreement.
- Confirm one experiment is reversible and has an undo.
- Verify the answer does not confuse compression with automation, arrangement, saturation, limiting, or EQ.
- Verify arrangement guidance names focal role and preservation goal.
- Verify frequency guidance tests level/register/timing/role before permanent carving.
- Confirm no model or corpus item gains Logic mutation authority.
