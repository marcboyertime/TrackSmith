# Source and knowledge policy

## Purpose

Forum knowledge is useful because it contains real language, follow-up context, failed attempts, exceptions, disagreements, and practical troubleshooting. It is not automatically reliable merely because it is repeated or upvoted.

## Ingestion rules

1. Do not distribute raw forum post bodies in TrackSmith’s shipped knowledge.
2. Preserve source URLs and source type.
3. Paraphrase the useful pattern.
4. Separate:
   - user-reported symptom;
   - proposed cause;
   - proposed experiment;
   - reported result;
   - unresolved limitation.
5. Keep contradictory recommendations visible.
6. Do not preserve universal frequency or threshold prescriptions as facts.
7. Exact Logic menu/control steps require Apple documentation and installed-version verification.
8. Numeric advice is a starting range or example unless a primary source documents product behavior.
9. Community anecdotes may seed retrieval and evaluation before they are approved as strategy knowledge.
10. A source may teach TrackSmith how users speak without being accepted as technical evidence.

## Reddit limitation

Reddit search and direct thread retrieval were inconsistent during this collection. One substantial thread was retrieved through an indexed mirror. That material is marked Tier C and used only in paraphrased form. No inaccessible search snippet was silently promoted into a knowledge record.

## Evidence tiers

### Tier A — primary/documentary

Official Apple Logic documentation or directly verified behavior.

Permitted use:
- control identity;
- documented function;
- version-scoped navigation;
- explicit product limitation.

Not permitted use:
- claiming a setting sounds better;
- universal artistic advice.

### Tier B — specialist practice

Retrieved Logic Pro Help or Gearspace discussions with enough surrounding context to understand the problem and response.

Permitted use:
- candidate diagnostic branches;
- candidate first experiments;
- known failure modes;
- real workflow language.

Requires:
- explicit context and limitation;
- human review before trusted knowledge promotion.

### Tier C — community anecdote

Broad community discussion or individual report.

Permitted use:
- utterance paraphrases;
- hypothesis candidates;
- disagreement records;
- adversarial/evaluation cases;
- examples of cargo-cult advice to avoid.

Not permitted use:
- exact instruction authority;
- universal numeric claims;
- automatic promotion to professional consensus.
