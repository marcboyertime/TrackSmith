# Candidate review checklist

For every claim, strategy, or procedure promoted from this package:

- [ ] A named reviewer and date are recorded.
- [ ] The source URL is retrievable or the limitation is explicit.
- [ ] The paraphrase accurately reflects the source pattern.
- [ ] No universal rule is inferred from one singer, mix, or forum answer.
- [ ] Numeric guidance is labeled as a starting point unless documentary.
- [ ] Competing causes remain visible.
- [ ] One discriminating experiment is available.
- [ ] What-to-listen-for is concrete.
- [ ] Stop rule and undo are safe and understandable.
- [ ] Non-DSP/source/arrangement alternatives are considered.
- [ ] Exact Logic labels and paths were checked on the installed version.
- [ ] “Can’t find it” fallback exists.
- [ ] The record does not claim TrackSmith heard, saw, or changed anything.
- [ ] The response can be stated in natural, concise producer language.
- [ ] The teaching principle transfers beyond the immediate setting.
