## Coverage design

Apple documentation is used for:

- documented Logic controls and behavior;
- Channel EQ, Analyzer, Compressor, DeEsser 2;
- Flex Pitch and pitch quantization;
- take folders and Quick Swipe Comping;
- monitoring latency and plug-in delay;
- MIDI Classic/Smart Quantize;
- Region inspector and advanced quantization;
- audio Flex/quantize parameters.

Logic Pro Help is used for:

- real failure reports;
- routing and monitoring problems;
- Flex analysis failures;
- timing/tempo-map ambiguity;
- artifact and workflow exceptions.

Gearspace is used for:

- practical vocal space/delay workflows;
- source-first versus processing-first disagreements;
- reverb filtering, predelay, ducking, and vocal-chain variety.

Reddit is used narrowly for:

- real musician phrasing;
- common beginner assumptions;
- explicit disagreements around reverb, delay, layering, compression, automation, and source quality.

## Known limitation

Direct Reddit retrieval/search was inconsistent. The package includes one large, retrievable vocal-production discussion through an indexed mirror rather than pretending to have a complete Reddit scrape. This limitation is explicit in the source registry and source policy.

## What additional collection should prioritize

After the current package is integrated and evaluated, new source collection should target gaps revealed by real Tutor sessions, especially:

- contemporary genre-specific vocal production;
- current Logic 12 UI changes;
- more direct, fully retrievable Reddit threads;
- advanced vocal automation and editing edge cases;
- quantization questions involving live orchestral and complex MIDI expression;
- bilingual/non-English musician phrasing if relevant.
