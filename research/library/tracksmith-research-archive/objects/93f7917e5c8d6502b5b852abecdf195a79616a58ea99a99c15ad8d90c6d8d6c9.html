Ozone 12 user guide

Abstract

Learn how to use Ozone 12. Find out about all its workflows, features, and controls.

Introduction

Go beyond the limits of mastering with Ozone 12. Unlock the impossible with this complete suite of 20 pro modules, including 3 brand-new, best-in-class additions. Get your low end right in any listening environment with Bass Control – an industry first in easy visualization, balancing, and shaping. Revive crushed mixes with Unlimiter, a groundbreaking “undo” button for overly compressed audio. Fine-tune vocals, bass, drums, and more with Stem EQ – all without having to open up your mix. Make Master Assistant your own with a new customized flow. And shape your loudest, cleanest masters yet with Maximizer’s new IRC 5 algorithm. With intelligent tech that guides, not decides, you're always in control.

Built on two decades of award-winning innovation at the cutting edge of mastering tech, this complete suite helps bring tracks to life – whether it's your first or the latest in a long line of hits.

Way back in 2017, Ozone's AI-powered Master Assistant sparked the shift to faster, smarter, more accessible mastering. Using the analysis of thousands of pro tracks, Master Assistant was the first to create custom presets that match target sounds in seconds. And it’s just that, your creative co-pilot, your trusty guide. There to save you precious time, to support your creativity, to take the mystery out of mastering.

Doing the impossible is an Ozone tradition. Best-in-class neural nets unlock new ways to master faster and smarter than ever, with uncharted levels of control and creativity. Need a little magic to fix a squashed mix? Some surgical control over stems? Ozone is the answer.

Whether you're starting your journey or perfecting your craft, there’s an Ozone just for you. Take your first steps with Elements, finesse your sound with Standard, or complete your suite with Advanced.
