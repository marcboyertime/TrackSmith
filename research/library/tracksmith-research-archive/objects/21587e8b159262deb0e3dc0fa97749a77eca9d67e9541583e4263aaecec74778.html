FAST plugins are discontinued

  • 27 February 2026 14:44
  • Updated

We have discontinued the FAST plugins to focus on releasing other music making tools and other partnerships. We do not plan any further updates for the FAST plugins.

You can no longer purchase any FAST plugins or the FAST Bundle.

We will continue to support FAST plugins until 16th February 2026. 

Focusrite continues to include essential tools that bring big studio sound to your songs with our products.
