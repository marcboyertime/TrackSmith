# Quality Boundaries

- Nonlinear processing facts do not prove that a source sounds warm, analog, expensive, or better.
- Spectral change does not prove artistic value.
- A louder processed signal is not a fair comparison.
- Oversampling can reduce aliasing but is not a universal quality switch.
- Product model names do not establish circuit equivalence.
- Transient-shaper labels vary by implementation.
- Attack/sustain changes can alter peak and average level, phase relationships, bleed, noise, and groove.
- Frequency-selective and multiband tools add detector/filter/crossover tradeoffs.
- Source, performance, arrangement, timing, level, and editing alternatives should be tested before extreme processing.
- Community and professional-practice guidance is contextual and may disagree.
- The owner’s listening confirmation remains decisive for preference and improvement.
