## Source hierarchy

1. Apple Logic documentation supplies version-scoped behavior and navigation candidates.
2. Professional-practice sources supply workflow, listening, and musical tradeoffs.
3. Specialist discussions supply troubleshooting language and concrete failure cases.
4. Community seeds supply natural phrasing and recurring disagreement, not truth authority.

## Storage policy

- No full forum page or raw discussion archive is included.
- `source_registry.jsonl` stores metadata, URLs, scope, limitations, and access mode.
- `provenance_manifest.jsonl` links each canonical synthesis to source IDs.
- Long verbatim source text is not redistributed.
- Model-facing records normally omit usernames.
