# Quality Boundaries

- BPM is not complete tempo understanding: downbeat, meter, beat phase, drift, and authority direction also matter.
- Smart Tempo analysis is candidate evidence until previewed against the music.
- File tempo metadata may be wrong.
- Bars and Beats are not interchangeable.
- A project tempo map can change while reference audio remains natural; audio stretching is a separate state.
- Half/double tempo and compound meter are musically ambiguous, not merely detector failures.
- Detailed maps can preserve rubato or create jitter; simplified maps can improve usability or erase expression.
- Exact Logic procedures are candidate-only and version-gated.
- The Tutor never executes Logic actions.
