# Quality Boundaries

- A sidechain trigger is not automatically audible in the target path.
- Automation mode and automation ownership are separate questions.
- Randomization is not musical feel by itself.
- Quantize values, Q-Strength, Q-Range, swing, and groove templates have different functions.
- Equal velocity values do not guarantee equal perceived loudness.
- MIDI Transform selection and operation must be verified separately.
- Popular workflows are not official Logic behavior.
- Candidate procedures remain unverified until checked on the installed version.
- The runtime projection contains concepts and diagnostic patterns, not procedures or navigation.
