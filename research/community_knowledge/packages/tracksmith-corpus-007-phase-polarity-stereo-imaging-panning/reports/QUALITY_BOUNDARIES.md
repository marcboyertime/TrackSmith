# Quality Boundaries

- Polarity inversion is not a universal 180-degree phase-alignment operation for complex audio.
- Phase claims require a named related signal or reference.
- A correlation meter is a diagnostic, not a quality score or universal target.
- Brief negative correlation is not automatically failure; critical mono loss must be established by listening.
- Width, balance, pan position, depth, and loudness are separate dimensions.
- Logic Balance and Stereo Pan are not interchangeable.
- Mid/Side components are not isolated instruments.
- Sample alignment can improve impact and also erase intentional depth.
- Low-frequency stereo is contextual; no fixed mono crossover is universal.
- Hard panning, asymmetry, and stereo compromise can be valid artistic choices.
- All numeric starting points are orientations, not presets.
- Candidate procedures are not installed-version proof and carry no execution authority.
