# Quality Boundaries

- MIDI controller names and defaults do not guarantee an instrument uses them in the same way.
- Visual grids and meters do not prove musical correctness.
- Bounce/export settings are destination-specific, not universal.
- Freeze and Bounce differ in project structure and editability.
- PDC, monitoring latency, and Recording Delay solve different timing problems.
- Track, region, channel strip, and audio-file ownership must be established before instructions.
- Exact Logic navigation is candidate-only until verified on the installed version.
