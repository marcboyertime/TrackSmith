# Quality boundaries

- Candidate synthesis is not reviewed product truth.
- A waveform, grid, source-separation estimate, or model output does not prove musical improvement.
- Crossfade length, denoise amount, alignment strength, layer count, crossover point, and pan/width settings are context-dependent.
- Editing should preserve source identity and rollback.
- Layering should be evaluated by perceptual role and context, not track count.
- Exact Logic guidance remains version-scoped and unverified until directly tested.
- Forum/professional anecdotes do not become consensus automatically.
