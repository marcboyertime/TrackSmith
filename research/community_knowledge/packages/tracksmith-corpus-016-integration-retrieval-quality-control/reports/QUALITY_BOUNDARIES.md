# Quality Boundaries

- Package 016 does not certify the truth of Packages 001–015.
- It preserves candidate review states and prevents silent promotion.
- Exact retrieval fixtures prove only deterministic identity retrieval, not semantic quality.
- Ambiguous queries are diagnostic-only and should trigger contextual reasoning or clarification.
- Public audio datasets can test known manipulations, modality receipt, timing, separation, noise and meter behavior; they do not replace owner judgment of artistic success.
- Synthetic or source-separated audio must be labeled as such.
- A model's self-confidence is not empirical calibration.
- The model must not see evaluation answers, candidate procedures or Logic navigation through the runtime projection.
