# Quality Boundaries

- A bus number is not a musical role.
- A send, output route, Group, Stack, and VCA do not grant interchangeable behavior.
- Popular routing practice is not official Logic behavior.
- A louder result is not automatically better.
- Shared processing does not automatically create cohesion or “glue.”
- Candidate Logic paths remain unverified until checked on the installed version.
- The live runtime projection contains concepts and diagnostic patterns, not procedures or navigation.
