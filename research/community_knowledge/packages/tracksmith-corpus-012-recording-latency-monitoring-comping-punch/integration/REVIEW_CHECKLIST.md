# Review Checklist

- Confirm the installed Logic version before promoting any procedure.
- Preserve native, original-review, original-verification, Logic-verification, and runtime-eligibility statuses separately.
- Verify hardware/interface-specific claims against manufacturer documentation.
- Treat monitoring, recorded placement, and performer timing as separate diagnostic dimensions.
- Preserve original takes and project alternatives before flattening, merging, replacing, or offsetting.
- Confirm mono/stereo input format against the actual connected channels.
- Never treat one buffer, Recording Delay offset, monitoring path, comp boundary, or punch range as universal.
- Keep every candidate procedure `execution_authority: false`.
