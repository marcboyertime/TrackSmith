# Quality Boundaries

- Meter activity does not prove audibility or recording.
- An audible direct cue does not prove Logic is recording.
- Monitoring latency, performer timing, and recorded-region placement are separate.
- I/O buffer size is not complete round-trip latency.
- Input gain and track-fader level are separate stages.
- A single microphone normally maps to one mono input; stereo is not a quality upgrade.
- Quick Swipe changes a comp decision; stronger flatten/merge actions require preserved originals.
- Crossfades cannot make fundamentally incompatible takes identical.
- Count-in and pre-roll have different purposes.
- A narrow punch range is not automatically more precise musically.
- Candidate procedures are not runtime knowledge and have no execution authority.
- Community and professional practices remain contextual rather than universal.
