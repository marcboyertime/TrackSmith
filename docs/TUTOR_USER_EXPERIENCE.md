# Tutor user experience

The Tutor is conversation first: a compact context bar states capture and listening provenance, assistant cards expose evidence and limitations, and one experiment card offers Listen/Show Me plus reversible outcome choices. Show Me remains a noninteractive read-only overlay with written fallback. The composer supports cancellation, offline fallback, accessibility labels, and a compact disclosure that local measurements are not model listening.

Before/after availability appears only after the musician confirms the edit was upstream of and observable at the measurement tap. If that condition or capture continuity is missing, the interface keeps the reported outcome and explains why deterministic comparison is unavailable.
