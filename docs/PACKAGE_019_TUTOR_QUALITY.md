# Package 019 Tutor quality calibration

Package 019 freezes a 240-case, evaluation-only Tutor suite before further quality tuning: 96 development, 72 calibration, and 72 held-out cases. Labels include diagnosis/experiment families, evidence boundaries, clarification and reviewed-navigation needs, authority prohibitions, stop/undo expectations, alternatives, and ambiguity/abstention expectations. They are not bundled, indexed, sent to a provider, or exposed to runtime tools.

The deterministic harness is `research/scripts/package19_quality_suite.py`. It verifies the suite hash, validates all three sanitized tracked cloud artifacts fail closed (including repeated-artifact tamper rejection), scans runtime resources (or an explicit built/installed bundle) for evaluation leakage, and emits bounded reproducible reports under `docs/evidence/`. No-tool completed 36 generation samples and 12 valid supporting judgments in 48 provider requests, with no tools. Full-tool is honest partial evidence: 26 completed samples, 6 fallback-excluded samples, 4 failed samples, and 5 valid supporting judgments in 142 provider requests; it has no timeouts. Repeated triplets is honest partial evidence: 81 completed samples, 23 fallback-excluded samples (22 tool-round-limit engine failures and one timeout), 4 malformed/no-usable-response failures, and 18 valid supporting judgments out of 36 slots (8/7/3 by repetition) in 446 provider requests. Its valid-judgment true counts are 14/18 evidence honesty, 17/18 usefulness, 9/18 experiment completeness, 7/18 strict level invariance, and 3/18 exact procedure necessity. The report exposes only counts, hashes, statuses, and cost snapshots—not assistant/judge prose, expected answers, credentials, hidden reasoning, or runtime-ineligible material. The full-tool worktree patch hash is `unavailable` only for its immutable pre-fix artifact, whose commit and index-tree identities are present; all future unavailable patch provenance is rejected. Repeated triplets is pinned to its immutable artifact SHA-256, source commit/index-tree, and clean-patch SHA-256. No-tool's 1,447,600 microUSD, full-tool's 6,453,608 microUSD, and repeated-triplets' 22,530,152 microUSD values are historical ledger snapshots, not exact confirmed provider spend: those artifacts lack cache-classified usage detail. The latter preserves the $50 cap, 18,371,320 microUSD retained reservations, and the earlier 9,888,608 microUSD external unknown hold. A newly consented run under cache-classified accounting is required before an exact provider-spend claim; it is not required for the existing qualitative artifact status. Cloud audio, installed app, Logic, and owner-listening evidence remain not run; full-tool and repeated-triplets supporting judgments are model-assisted but partial, not complete quality measurements. The executable text harnesses are `make p19-cloud-no-tool`, `make p19-cloud-full-tool`, and `make p19-cloud-repeated-triplets`; each requires `CLOUD_TEXT_CONSENT=YES`, `CLOUD_BUDGET_CAP_USD=50`, and the single private ledger `~/Library/Application Support/TrackSmith/Evaluations/package019-cloud-budget-v1.json`, initialized exactly once with `CLOUD_BUDGET_EXTERNAL_UNKNOWN_HOLD_MICROUSD=9888608 make p19-cloud-budget-init`. `CLOUD_BUDGET_LEDGER` is optional only as an equality assertion of that exact canonical path; arbitrary paths fail closed. Before every forwarding request, the local ledger locks and reserves the conservative exact-request-byte input bound plus the configured 5,000-token output maximum at the pinned gpt-5.6-sol priority/Fast price, charging `input_tokens_details.cache_write_tokens` at 10 microUSD short-context or 20 microUSD long-context (1.25x uncached input) and cached reads at 1 or 2 microUSD; it retains reservations after errors, unsupported tiers, untrusted usage, or cost overflow and settles only a completed response with reconciled uncached, cache-read, and cache-write usage. The ledger contains counts, caps, and accounting only—never credentials, request/prose payloads, hidden reasoning, or evaluation labels. The immutable 9,888,608 micro-USD external unknown hold was recorded under the superseded 16 microUSD long-context input assumption; it remains retained without recalculation and is not a current cache-write reservation or exact-spend claim. These harnesses use the production provider path with `store:false` and are not invoked by Package 019 deterministic gates.

Runtime retrieval keeps the Package 018 reader and now distinguishes a valid `noMatch` from `queryFailed`, `malformedSelectedPayload`, `schemaDrift`, `corrupt`, `disabled`, `unavailable`, and `versionMismatch`. Those sanitized outcomes do not expose local paths or SQLite errors. Ranking and abstention are separate calibration measurements; ambiguity diagnostics remain evaluation-only/model-withheld until held-out balanced accuracy is at least 0.75.

The LLM remains the primary reasoner and author. The existing compact policy and presentation-only `present_experiment` contract require one user-performed, reversible experiment with a baseline/start, one variable, listen cue, risk, stop/undo, and honest evidence boundary when an actionable turn warrants it. Pure explanation or necessary clarification is not force-templated.

Packages 005 and 006 historical import reports remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Additive Package 019 attestations supply their missing portable `force_semantics:false` projections for clean-checkout verification only.

Run `make p19-ci` for deterministic gates, or `make p19-built-resource-scan BUNDLE=/path/to/TrackSmith.app` for an explicit built/installed bundle. See [the machine report](evidence/PACKAGE_019_FINAL_REPORT.json) for the exact evidence boundary.
